Privacy Policy

AquaTide: Block Puzzle  |  Last updated: November 15, 2026

In short
  • You do not need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number inside the game.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses analytics (Google Firebase). These services collect some device information, listed below.
  • Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent, and you can change your choice in the game settings.

1. Who we are

AquaTide: Block Puzzle (“the game”) is published by Origins ID (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at playbyorigins@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

The game saves the following on your device so it can work: your best score, your sound, music and vibration settings, whether you finished the tutorial, your unfinished game (so you can continue it), and some counters used to decide when to show ads (for example how many games you finished and on how many days you played). We do not receive this information. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

3. Information collected by our partners

The game uses two services from Google. They collect information automatically when you use the game. We do not control exactly how they work, but we describe what they tell us they collect.

ServiceWhat it may collectWhy
Google AdMob (ads) IP address (which can show your approximate location), Android advertising ID and app set ID, how you interact with ads and the app (for example app launch, taps and video views), and diagnostic information such as app launch time and performance. Showing ads, measuring ads, preventing fraud, and analytics.
Google Firebase Analytics An app-instance ID, basic device information (such as model, operating system version and language), approximate location derived from your IP address, and the events described below. Understanding how the game is used so we can fix problems and make it better.

The events we send to Firebase Analytics are about gameplay, not about you as a person. They include: starting and finishing a game (with the score, how long it lasted, how many blocks were placed and the difficulty level), progress through the tutorial, offers to continue a game and whether you accepted them, difficulty level changes, and how many ads were shown. We do not send your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and we do not set a user ID.

All of this data is sent to Google over encrypted connections.

4. How we use the information

  • To show ads, which is how the game is paid for.
  • To understand how many people play, how long they play and where they stop, so we can improve the game.
  • To balance difficulty and fix problems.

5. Your choices and consent

  • Consent form. In regions where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game shows a consent form from Google before ads are requested. If it applies to you, a Privacy button appears in the game’s Settings, where you can review or change your choice.
  • Advertising ID. On your Android device you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in the device’s Google or Ads settings.
  • Uninstall. Removing the game stops all collection by the game.

Ads may be personalized or not, depending on where you live and the choices you make.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your information ourselves. The information in section 3 is collected by and shared with Google as described above. We do not share anything else with other companies. We may disclose information if a law or a valid legal request requires it.

7. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data held by Google is kept according to Google’s own retention rules and the settings we choose in the Firebase and AdMob consoles.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game, please contact us so we can look into it.

9. Where data is processed

Google may process data in countries other than your own. By using the game you understand this, and where required we rely on the safeguards Google provides.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR, or Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law), you may have the right to ask what data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not collect your name or other direct identifiers, we may be unable to find your data without extra information such as your advertising ID. Contact us at playbyorigins@gmail.com and we will do what we reasonably can. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is important, we will also tell you in the game.

12. In-app purchases

AquaTide: Block Puzzle offers one optional in-app purchase, Remove Ads. It removes banner, interstitial and app-open ads, and gives extra free uses of the in-game boosts in every game. Rewarded ads, which you choose to watch, stay available.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card or bank details. Through Google Play Billing (used via Unity's In-App Purchasing software), the app receives confirmation that a purchase was made, for example the product ID and a purchase receipt, and uses it to unlock the feature. The app checks this confirmation each time it opens, so Remove Ads can be restored if you reinstall the app or use a new device with the same Google account. The app also keeps a simple "Remove Ads purchased" setting on your device, so the feature keeps working when you are offline.

Your payment method, purchase history and refunds are handled by Google under Google Play's own terms and privacy policy. Refund requests are made through Google Play.

To understand how the game is used, we log anonymous events such as whether a purchase was started, completed or failed. These events do not contain your name, email address or payment details.
